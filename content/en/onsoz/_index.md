## Why BrailleQuran?

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah,
Lord of all the worlds. May peace and blessings be upon our Prophet (peace be upon him),
his Companions and his household.\
1- Read in the name of your Lord who created.\
2- He created man from a clot of blood.\
3- Read, and your Lord is the Most Generous.\
4- He who taught by the pen.\
5- Taught man that which he knew not. [^1]

This page presents the Braille system. Even though the Elif juz is available in the
Braille editions of the Noble Quran, a qualified teacher who is proficient in Braille
and able to teach it may not always be found. Moreover, a Braille Elif juz does not
exist in every language. Indeed, the Quran Braille system shown and taught in this
booklet has been prepared for sighted people, that is, both to help the visually
impaired and to be able to learn Quran Braille on one's own. In this matter, a sighted
teacher will not be able to replace a visually impaired teacher. However, in cases where
a visually impaired teacher cannot be found, a fully sighted teacher can enable a
visually impaired student to read the Quran. The Noble Quran is distributed worldwide by
various institutions. Even if a visually impaired student possesses a Quran mushaf
prepared in Braille, they will ask themselves, "How can I read the Quran?" They will
then inquire and, at best, find a teacher or strive to learn on their own. If the student
cannot learn on their own or cannot find a visually impaired teacher, their love for the
Quran may perhaps be harmed. Furthermore, different Braille systems are used in different
countries. This booklet presents two different systems: the one used in Türkiye and the
one used in Saudi Arabia and Kuwait. The second system is used, for example, in the
mushafs found at the Prophet's Mosque (Masjid an-Nabawi).

God willing, this work will reach more visually impaired brothers and sisters, become a
means for them to learn the Quran, and bring great benefit and contribution to the Ummah
of Muhammad (peace be upon him).
