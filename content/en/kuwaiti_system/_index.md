## Additional Information


The Braille system used in Saudi Arabia and Kuwait is almost identical to the Turkish
Braille language.

The points that are exactly the same are as follows. These points are the same as the
lessons covered in the second part:

1.  The Arabic alphabet

2.  The vowel marks (harakat)

3.  Sukun and Jazm

4.  The Hamza variants

5.  Ta Marbuta


## Madd Elongations

In the Arabic system the Braille form of the Madd elongations is the same as in the
Turkish system. However, in the Arabic system there are no hidden Madd elongations.
Instead, only a vowel is placed.

In addition, in the Arabic Braille system the vowels of the letters preceding the
ا, ي and و elongations are written before those elongations.

## Tanwin

Even though Tanwin is written the same way in both systems, there is a small detail
here. The Fatha Tanwin is usually written together with an Alif. In the Turkish system
the Fatha Tanwin is written after the Alif, whereas in the Arabic system it is the
opposite.

{{< datatable src="kuwaiti_system/tenvin" headers="Word|Turkish Braille|Arabic Braille System" >}}

## Alif Elongations

Alif-Madd and Alif Maqsura are written the same way in both systems, and furthermore
the Waw-Alif elongation is not used in the Arabic system. An Alif is written instead.
