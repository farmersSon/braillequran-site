## Lesson 8: Ta Marbuta (Round Ta)

It appears at the end of words and indicates that the word is feminine. In Braille it is represented with ⠡(dots 1,6). Unlike the open Ta, when stopping at the end of the word it transforms into a silent Ha; when continuing, it functions as a vowelized Ta.
